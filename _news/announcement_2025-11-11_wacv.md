Our paper:

**Karol Wojtulewicz, Minxing Liu, and Niklas Carlsson,  
“Advancing Player Identification and Tracking with Global ID Fusion (GIF)”**,  
has been accepted to the **IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV)**,  
to be held in Tucson, Arizona, in **March 2026**.

The paper proposes a Global ID Fusion (GIF) approach that enhances multi-object player identification and tracking across complex sports video sequences.

I will attend the conference in March 2026 — photos and updates will be added here after the trip!
