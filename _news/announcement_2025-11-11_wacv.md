Our paper **“Advancing Player Identification and Tracking with Global ID Fusion (GIF)”**, co-authored by Karol Wojtulewicz, Minxing Liu, and Niklas Carlsson, has been accepted to the **IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) 2026**, to be held in Tucson, Arizona.

The paper proposes a Global ID Fusion (GIF) approach that enhances multi-object player identification and tracking across complex sports video sequences. I plan to attend the conference in March 2026, and photos and further updates will be added here after the trip.
