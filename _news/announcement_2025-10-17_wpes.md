I am delighted to announce that our paper: **[“From Privacy Chains to ChainShield: Structured Privacy Risks and Defense in Vision-Language Models”](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3733802.3764048)** was accepted to the **[24th Workshop on Privacy in the Electronic Society (WPES)](http://jianying.space/WPES2025/accepted.html)** held in conjuction with **[ACM CCS 2025](https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2025/)**.

The paper introduces a structured analysis of how multi-step VQA pipelines can leak sensitive information, forming what we call *privacy chains*. We also propose **ChainShield**, a white-box adversarial defense that perturbs images to reduce privacy leakage across VLMs.

I attended WPES 2025 and presented our work. It was a great experience!
